## Who (Ideal Customer Profile)

The vendor who feels the pain **today** and is nimble enough to switch:

- **Already sells on WhatsApp** — has a customer base living in their chats and
  posts to Status. (Hard gate. If they don't sell on WhatsApp yet, they're not
  beta material.)
- **Physical, photographable goods**: Ankara / fashion, beauty & cosmetics,
  shoes & bags, food, phone accessories. (Services and B2B-only: not now.)
- **AOV 3,000–30,000 XOF**, **~10–100 orders/month** — enough volume to feel the
  pain (lost orders, repeating "c'est combien?", chasing payment), small enough
  to adopt fast.
- **Uses T-Money or Flooz** already.
- **Smartphone + daily WhatsApp.**
- **In or near Lomé** for the beta (support proximity, in-person onboarding).
- **Bonus, high-leverage:** a "WhatsApp/Status influencer-vendor" with a
  following, or a **market-association leader** (one yes → many, and the
  cooperative feature signs the whole group).

**Disqualifiers:** services-only, no smartphone, sells only via physical shop
foot-traffic (no WhatsApp habit), wholesale-only B2B.

## Screening scorecard (qualify in 60 seconds)

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Sells on WhatsApp now | gate — must be yes |
| ≥10 orders/month | 2 |
| Uses T-Money/Flooz | 2 |
| Posts products to Status/groups | 1 |
| Has product photos ready | 1 |
| Willing to try 30 days + give feedback | 2 |
| Association leader / has a following | +2 bonus |

Take vendors scoring **6+**. Aim for a mix: ~70% solo vendors, ~30% leaders/
influencers (the multipliers).

## Where to find them (warmest → coldest)

1. **Your own network + referrals** — warmest, highest conversion. Ask every
   yes for two more.
2. **Market-association / "groupement" leaders** — Grand Marché, Assivito,
   Adawlato, Hédzranawoé. One intro unlocks a roster; the **cooperative onboarding
   feature is the wedge** (sign the whole association with one join link).
3. **Existing WhatsApp vendor groups** — you're already in some; post a soft
   "looking for 20 vendors to test a free tool" call.
4. **Status/TikTok micro-vendors** — DM the ones visibly selling in Status.
5. **Mobile-money agents** — they know who's transacting; a referral channel.

Bias hard toward **warm intros** — cold DMs to micro-vendors convert poorly and
burn trust.

## The pitch (lead with pain + zero risk)

Script (French, with Ewe warmth — adapt to the person):

> « Bonjour [name] 👋 Je vois que tu vends sur WhatsApp. Tu perds parfois des
> commandes dans les messages, ou tu répètes “c'est combien ?” toute la journée ?
> On a fait **Japora** : ton catalogue + un robot qui répond aux prix, et le
> client paie en T-Money/Flooz dans la conversation. **C'est gratuit, tu paies
> seulement quand tu vends.** Je t'installe ça en 15 minutes, on teste 30 jours,
> et tu me dis franchement si c'est utile. Ça te dit ? »

Principles:
- **Pain first**, product second. ("lost orders / repeating prices / chasing
  payment" — name the one that's theirs.)
- **Zero risk** up front: free, no app to install, pay only on a sale.
- **White-glove onboarding**: you (or an agent) set up their catalog *with* them
  in 15 minutes — import their WhatsApp catalog or add 5 products together. Don't
  hand a micro-vendor a dashboard and walk away.
- **Time-boxed ask**: "30 days, honest feedback" — low commitment, signals you
  want the truth.

## Onboarding funnel + success metrics

| Stage | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruited** | Agreed to try | 15–30 |
| **Activated** | Shop created + ≥3 products + catalog link shared | ≥80% of recruited |
| **Aha** | First real order **through Japora** + first inline payment | ≥60% of activated |
| **Retained (wk 4)** | Still placing orders in week 4; opens the 6 PM summary | ≥50% of activated |
| **Love** | "How would you feel if you could no longer use Japora?" → **"very disappointed" ≥ 40%** (Sean Ellis PMF test) | the real signal |

Instrument the funnel (BotEvents, orders, summary opens already give you most of
it). **Talk to every vendor weekly** — 15-minute calls beat any dashboard for a
30-person beta.

## 4-week beta plan

- **Week 0 — Prep:** finalize pricing copy in XOF, register WhatsApp templates,
  recruit 20–30 from warm channels, build the tracker (below).
- **Week 1 — Onboard:** white-glove setup, get each to *activated*. Watch for
  drop-off at "add products" — that's where micro-vendors stall; do it for them.
- **Week 2 — First sales:** push for the *aha* (first Japora-routed paid order).
  Daily check-ins with anyone stuck.
- **Week 3 — Habit:** is the 6 PM summary landing? Are they sharing the catalog
  link in Status? Fix the top-3 friction points you've heard.
- **Week 4 — Measure + decide:** run the PMF survey, the funnel numbers, and
  qualitative themes. Decide: expand, pivot the wedge, or fix-and-rerun.
