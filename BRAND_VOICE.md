# Japora — voice & tone

A short guide. Read it once. Refer back when copy feels off.

## The synthesis

Japora sells to a vendor whose day is **loud** — DMs at 6am, deliveries by lunch,
M-Pesa pings until midnight. Japora's voice is the **calm** in that. Picture a
great executive assistant: the client is wired, the assistant is unflappable.

**The vendor hustles. Japora handles.**

That phrase — *quietly capable hustle* — resolves the tension between Japora's
WhatsApp-first energy and Aurora's quiet-luxury surface.

---

## Principles

1. **Customer as hero.** Lead with *you*, not *us*. Not "Japora answers your
   customers." → "Your customers get a reply." Better: "Your bot says hi.
   You stay in WhatsApp."

2. **Quietly capable.** No hype words. No exclamation marks. No "powerful",
   "seamless", "unleash", "revolutionize". A confident sentence doesn't need
   an exclamation point.

3. **Concrete over abstract.** Numbers, names, places. "1% capped at ₦200"
   not "fair pricing". "M-Pesa, Paystack, Flutterwave" not "African payment
   rails". "Auntie Nkechi" not "your customer base".

4. **Present tense, active verbs.** "Japora answers" not "Japora will respond to".
   "Get paid" not "Receive payment". One verb per sentence when possible.

5. **Short, then medium, then earned-long.** Sentence rhythm. Like good FT
   prose. Not all snappy fragments — that gets tiresome. Vary.

6. **Lagos-to-Lagos.** Written like a friend who's done it. Not a Stanford MBA
   describing it. Knows what M-Pesa STK Push looks like. Knows what a "tab"
   is. Knows that nobody answers "How much?" at 11 PM.

7. **Microcopy is jokes-adjacent, never jokes.** "Got it." not "Awesome!".
   "tonight" not "soon ✨". "We didn't catch that" not "Oops!".

8. **Default to less.** Cut the qualifier. Cut the hedge. Cut the second
   sentence if the first did the work.

## Vocabulary

### Words we use

- *hustle* — keep, owns the brand
- *handle* — the verb that anchors the voice ("Japora handles", "we handle")
- *get paid* — never "receive payment" or "accept payments"
- *tonight*, *tomorrow*, *today* — concrete time over vague time
- *your shop*, *your shelf*, *your tab* — possessive anchors
- *quietly* — the watchword

### Words we don't

- *seamless*, *powerful*, *unleash*, *empower*, *robust*, *unlock*,
  *revolutionize*, *elevate*, *leverage*, *transform*
- *amazing*, *awesome*, *incredible* — never. "Got it." is enough.
- *just* (as filler) — "just type your email" → "type your email"
- *simply* — same as above
- "the future of X" — no
- exclamation points — almost never. A single one in a 50-page app is fine.

## Tone matrix

Different moments. Different temperatures.

| Moment                  | Temperature  | Example                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hero / landing          | calm + warm  | "Your hustle, quietly handled."                                     |
| Pricing                 | matter-of-fact | "Free until you sell. Then 1%."                                   |
| Onboarding empty state  | encouraging  | "Your shelf is open. Add a product."                               |
| Success state           | quiet relief | "Got it. Order #A1B2 confirmed."                                   |
| Receivable reminder     | gentle, firm | "Hi Bola — gentle nudge. ₦7,500 outstanding since Friday."         |
| Error                   | accountable  | "We didn't catch that. Try again?" (not "Oops!" / "Something went wrong!") |
| Legal pages             | plain        | Drop the marketing. Say what it is.                                |
| Hustle Summary (6 PM)   | concise diary | "Today: 7 orders · ₦42,000 · 2 unpaid (Bola, Tunde)."             |

## Before / after

**Before**: "Japora is the WhatsApp-first storefront for African micro-entrepreneurs — list your products once, share a single catalog link, get paid through the rails your customers already use, and run the whole hustle from your phone."

**After**: "Japora keeps your WhatsApp shop running. One catalog link. M-Pesa and Paystack inline. A summary every evening. You stay in WhatsApp."

---

**Before**: "Awesome! 🎉 You've successfully created your shop. Welcome to the Japora family! 🚀"

**After**: "Your shop is live. Demo creds work — try them now, or share your link."

---

**Before**: "Reset your password instantly! Just click the magic link we sent to your inbox below ✨"

**After**: "Check your inbox. The link expires in an hour."

---

**Before**: "Powerful WhatsApp business automation that helps you seamlessly manage your customer interactions and unlock new revenue streams."

**After**: "Your bot answers price-checks. You handle the tricky ones."

## A note on emoji

Use sparingly as **icons**, not as **punctuation**.

- 📲 next to "WhatsApp bot" in a feature card: fine — it's the icon.
- 🎉 next to "Welcome!": no — it's punctuation. Cut it.
- 🌙 in "6 PM Hustle Summary tonight": fine — it carries calendar meaning.
- 🔥, 💯, 🚀, ✨: never. Period.

When in doubt, take the emoji out. Read the sentence. If the meaning survives,
the emoji was decorative — leave it out.

## A note on brand line

The official line is **"Sell on WhatsApp. Get paid. Grow your hustle."** Keep
it as the meta description, the footer line, the email wrapper signature.

Lead headlines (above the fold, page-title-level) should usually *not* be the
brand line — they should be the page's own hook in the same voice:

- Landing: "Your hustle, quietly handled."
- Pricing: "Free until you sell. Then 1%."
- Marketplace: "Discover WhatsApp shops near you."
- Shop profile: just the shop name.
- Bookkeeping: "The month, distilled."

The brand line is the chorus. The page-specific hook is the verse.
