## What it does for you without being asked

Parses a workbook and proposes the column mapping, drafts titles, descriptions and category mappings from a photo plus a two-word row, flags missing identifiers before a destination silently rejects them, compresses and re-hosts images with a nightly reachability crawl, recomputes collection membership on every feed change, regenerates the storefront and pings the sitemap, decrements stock on payment, and computes the quarterly reprice-and-retire shortlist.

## What it will always ask you first

- Nothing. This product takes no outward action on your behalf.

Every one of these is one tap, in WhatsApp, in your language, with the cost or the
consequence stated. The platform records who approved, when, and in which language
the question was asked.

- `ai-inference`
- `claim-screen`

- `infrastructure-capability`

## On a bad connection

First paint is budgeted at 150 KB over the wire.
Voice notes are capped at 300 KB per 60
seconds and product images at 100 KB,
because a merchant on a prepaid bundle pays for every one of them.
